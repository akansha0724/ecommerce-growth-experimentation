# Growth analytics — Online Retail II

- Transactions: **784,885** · customers: **5,859** · revenue: **GBP 16.8M** (2009-12-01 to 2011-12-09)

- **Retention is the core problem:** on average only **20%** of a cohort returns the month after acquisition, ~14% by month 6. Acquisition without retention leaks revenue.
- **Revenue concentrates in a few segments:** *Champions* are 25% of customers but 70% of revenue — the segment to protect.
- **Pareto:** the top 20% of customers drive **77%** of revenue — retention/loyalty spend should be concentrated there.
